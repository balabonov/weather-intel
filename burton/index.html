NWS Detroit/Pontiac · 715 AM EDT Sat Oct 24 2026

- The potential exists for dense fog development Sunday and Monday
  mornings. Any patchy/dense fog is expected to dissipate by around
  10 am each morning.

- High temperatures increase to around 70 on Sunday before a dry cold
  front drops us back into the upper 50s to low 60s Monday and
  Tuesday.

- Dry conditions through the middle of next week.

&&

.AVIATION...

Another round of fog and low stratus affects the terminal corridor 
this morning, although with less consistent coverage and/or depth 
compared to Friday morning. This helps LIFR restriction improve more 
quickly and with a lower/shorter duration stratus component, 
depending on any late development off western Lake Erie into the 
DTW/PTK areas. Observations leading up to forecast issuance are 
optimistic but will be monitored through the morning. 

Also similar to Friday is the dissipation of residual lower clouds 
by this afternoon. Model forecast soundings are very dry above the 
boundary layer which leaves clouds limited to bands of cirrus 
through the evening. A greater amount of opaque coverage toward MBS 
is a negative factor for more fog there late tonight. Both lower 
coverage of cirrus and a light south wind are more constructive for 
fog toward DTW by Sunday morning. 

D21/DTW Convection... Thunderstorms are not forecast through the TAF 
period.
