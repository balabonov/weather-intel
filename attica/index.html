NOTE
A cold front will sweep across lower MI today between about 18Z and 00Z bringing with it a scattered showers and gusty southwesterly winds. The front was meridional passing through MN and WI today with strong fgen and a strong low level jet feeding the front. As the surface low tracks from Lake Superior tonight up to western Quebec by this afternoon, it will drag the front through the area which will become increasingly more positively tilted. The fgen weakens and becomes disorganized and the low level jet lifts northeastward away from lower MI. A weak mid level wave will move into the area as the front passes this afternoon which will help daytime heating to bring very weak instability over the area with MUCAPE up to a couple hundred J/kg. Lapse rates are still in the 6-7 C/km range for the most part in the narrow moist layer ahead of the front. Expectations are for scattered showers to move into Saginaw Valley this morning before spreading south and east across the area with the front through the afternoon. A few embedded non-severe thunderstorms may accompany the showers, especially later this afternoon around the Detroit area as the front tries to re-intensify before exiting to the southeast. High temperatures will rise into the 70s once more today with the later frontal passage. The next ridge building in behind the front out of Canada will bring a cooler airmass with 850mb temps down to around 5C. Lows tonight will respond dropping into the mid to upper 30s for most locations away from the Detroit heat island. Highs Friday will then struggle to hit 60s, which is actually still above normal for late October. Mostly clear skies to start Friday night will allow for a quick drop in temps once again down to around freezing in the Thumb. Clouds will start filling back in across the south so will limit the cooling potential closer to the Ohio border so lows may struggle to drop below 40. The aforementioned clouds are associated with a southern stream front lifting into the Ohio Valley. Elevated portions will extend toward southern MI but strong surface high and overall upper level height rises will result in strong stability and prevent precip from making it into southeast MI. This ridging will hold into the new week keeping temperatures running above normal through this time. The next system slated to enter the region Tuesday. && .MARINE...
