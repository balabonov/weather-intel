.AVIATION...

A plume of deep layer moisture will continue to lift across Se Mi 
this afternoon in advance of a cold front. Area observations 
indicate widespread MVFR ceilings likely to prevail into the early 
evening. There have been observations showing IFR ceilings along a 
secondary low level moisture influx just to the southwest of the 
terminals, which may lead to some intervals of IFR ceilings this 
afternoon. There will likely be fluctuations in the frequency of 
wind gusts over 20kt this afternoon. The southerly gradient flow 
will sustain 30 knot winds down to 2k feet. A shallow near surface 
stable layer will suppress gusts at the surface periodically. The 
cold front will progress across Se Mi between 02Z and 03Z this 
evening, causing a wind shift to the southwest. Post frontal cold 
air advection will support increasing chances for ceiling heights to 
rise to VFR within the post frontal environment.

below 2000 feet through the afternoon into the evening. A wind shift 
from SSW to SW associated with the cold front is forecast between 
02Z and 03Z this evening. While ceilings are forecast to rise post 
frontal passage, current model guidance suggest they will remain aob 
5000 feet into the overnight.
