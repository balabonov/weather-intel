.AVIATION...

A strong and compact upper level wave will impact all of Lower 
Michigan today, with its current position near the Mackinac Straits. 
This low will quicky surge south, establishing strong cold advection 
across Lake Superior-Lake Michigan that sets up a long fetch for 
moisture flux. The main result of this locally will be a blanket of 
MVFR stratus today and tonight, aided by a strong mid-level dry slot 
aloft. A few showers noted on radar over northern MI may survive to 
MBS, although cloud cover/showers will tend to break up away from 
their moisture source. With that in mind, opted to include a TEMPO 
for occasional VFR conditions to mix in for DTW and nearby terminals 
this morning before daytime mixing establishes a healthy stratocu 
deck across the airspace. There still may be a few breaks in cloud 
cover this afternoon-evening as seen in some model guidance, but on 
too small of a scale to include in the TAF at this time. Otherwise, 
northwest flow becomes breezy this afternoon with gusts up to 20-25 
knots that may linger overnight depending on the track of the 
system's surface low. Models have trended east with the low 
trajectory, but if it does shift back west at all this would mean 
increased shower potential and reinforcement of gusty winds.
