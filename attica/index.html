.AVIATION...

Ridge of high pressure over the central Great Lakes region will 
continue to sink southward into the Ohio Valley through tonight and 
Sunday. The subsidence and dry air with this ridge will keep skies 
clear across most of Southeast MI through Sunday. As the ridge moves 
across SE MI and into the Ohio Valley, light and variable winds 
quickly become southwesterly this afternoon and continue into 
Sunday. A better gradient across the Tri Cities and Flint areas will 
keep a noticeably stronger wind overnight and allow for some 
gustiness on Sunday.
