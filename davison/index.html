.AVIATION...

High pressure centered over southern Quebec reaches into the Great 
Lakes with light NE wind today and tonight. The wind direction is 
the primary driver behind the LIFR fog and stratus this morning as 
moisture is swept inland from the warm water of Lake Huron. These 
restrictions persist along the terminal corridor until improving 
through IFR and MVFR mid to late morning. Late October daytime 
heating gradually dissipates remaining pockets of clouds in the 
lower range of VFR this afternoon. 

High pressure drifts slightly toward the Atlantic coast tonight 
which shifts the wind direction to SE into Lower Mi. There is a 
chance for more fog late tonight but with the wind direction 
limiting involvement from Lake Huron and favoring Lake Erie 
influence through sunrise Saturday.
