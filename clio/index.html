Ridging aloft and at the surface continue to dominate the region and will provide several more days of quiet weather around the Great Lakes. Ample moisture with longer cool nights brings areas of fog across the state, heavily influenced by wind direct deciding which locations get the fog and how dense. As the center of the surface drifts southward from southern Quebec toward the east coast of the conus, the surface flow will change from northeasterly to southeasterly today as we become positioned on the west side of the high.
